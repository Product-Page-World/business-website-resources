# Business Website Resources

Practical resources and best practices for planning, designing, developing, optimizing, and growing business websites.

This resource repository is maintained by **Product Page World**, the Technology & Digital Services business of **Shandilya TeleFinTech LLP**.

## Why Business Websites Matter

A business website can serve as an important digital foundation for communicating a company's services, products, expertise, and value to potential customers.

An effective website should combine useful information, clear navigation, responsive design, good user experience, and appropriate search optimization.

## Website Planning

Before developing a website, businesses should consider:

- Business objectives
- Target audience
- Website structure
- Required pages
- Services or product categories
- Content requirements
- User journeys
- Calls to action
- Mobile experience
- Search visibility

## Important Website Pages

Depending on the business, a professional website may include:

- Homepage
- About
- Services
- Individual service pages
- Products
- Product detail pages
- Contact
- FAQs
- Blog or resources
- Case studies
- Privacy policy
- Terms and conditions

The right structure depends on the business model, audience, and purpose of the website.

## Website User Experience

Useful website experiences generally focus on:

- Simple navigation
- Clear information hierarchy
- Readable content
- Responsive layouts
- Consistent design
- Clear calls to action
- Fast and usable pages
- Accessible content
- Easy contact options

## Website SEO Foundations

A search-friendly website should consider:

- Logical website architecture
- Descriptive URLs
- Useful page titles
- Relevant headings
- Helpful content
- Internal linking
- Image optimization
- Mobile usability
- Page performance
- Structured data where appropriate
- Crawlable links

SEO should support useful content and a good user experience rather than relying on excessive keyword repetition.

## Website Development

Website development can involve different technologies and platforms depending on the business requirements.

Common considerations include:

- Content management systems
- Responsive development
- Front-end implementation
- Back-end functionality
- Forms and integrations
- E-commerce functionality
- Security
- Performance
- Maintenance

## Website Growth

A website can work together with other digital channels, including:

- Search engine optimization
- Digital marketing
- Social media
- Content marketing
- Email marketing
- Paid advertising
- Online reputation
- Analytics and performance measurement
